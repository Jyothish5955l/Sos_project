💡 How Guardian Priority Tiers Work

Your contacts are organized into 3 protection rings for reliable escalation.

Tier 1 — Primary Immediate Alert

Immediate family (Mom, Dad, Spouse). Notified instantly on any SOS trigger with full Google Maps pin via cellular SMS.

Tier 2 — Secondary Escalation Alert

Close friends, roommates, or neighbors. Notified after 60s if Tier 1 does not acknowledge or on manual escalation.

Tier 3 — Backup Critical Fallback

Extended emergency contacts, local doctors, or trusted supervisors for high-severity alerts.

Choose between Single Alert dispatch or Multi-Tier Auto-Escalation.

Repeat Alert Auto-Escalation

Currently: Disabled (Single Alert Mode). When disabled, SOS Guardian dispatches exactly ONE urgent SMS with your Google Maps location to your primary guardians. No repeat messages will be sent.

💡 Tip: If you only want 1 message sent to your contacts, keep this switch OFF (Single Alert Mode). Turn it ON only if you want the system to automatically page backup contacts (Tier 2 and Tier 3) if you don't respond within 60 seconds.
